# Forkast design brief and PRD

The full brief lives in a Claude Doc:
https://claude.ai/artifact/TwpnQcpVHQGiahUr9Jb5RN

Forkast is a swipe-to-decide recipe app that helps home cooks escape the
"same five dinners" rut. It shows a broad range of recipes grounded in the
flavours each person already loves. The brief targets a hackday demo. The
primary customer is a confident cook who's bored of their rotation; couples
matching is a fun secondary bet. The demo MVP adds two ways to start ("tell me
what you like" or "go wild"), learning taste from swipes, and a "your taste"
view that plays back top flavours, ingredients and recipes. A year-end
"Wrapped" recap comes later. The current prototype is treated as an early
probe. The riskiest assumptions are that swipes reveal taste and that poor fit,
not too few recipes, is what keeps people in a rut. The brief includes an
interview guide and a small swipe test to check those before more building.
