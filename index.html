Never the same dinner twice.

Other apps give you the same recipes as everyone else. Forkast learns the flavours you love, finds new dishes that hit the same notes, and tweaks every recipe to suit you.

Let's get started Swipe a few dishes and Forkast learns your tastes

How Forkast is different

  1. Just swipe, no formsEvery save and skip teaches Forkast what you love. The first dishes are deliberately varied so it learns fast.
  2. Variety, not repeatsNew dishes and new cuisines, all grounded in the flavours you already love.
  3. Recipes rewritten for you"You love salty, savoury flavours, so we've added two extra anchovies." Not keen on something? It's swapped out.

How to choose

  • ♥Save: swipe right, or tap Save. It goes on your list.
  • ←Not now: swipe left. It'll come back later.
  • ✕Never: tap Never and you won't see it again.
  • ♥♥Love it? Double-tap the card to super like.
